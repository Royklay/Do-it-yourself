Frequently Asked Questions

What is FixItYourself?

FixItYourself is a platform that provides simple repair guides and interactive learning tools to help users solve common problems.

Do I need technical experience?

No. Our guides are designed to be easy to understand, even for beginners.

Does every category have a 3D model?

No. The computer category uses an interactive 3D model, while other categories provide step-by-step repair guides.

Can I fix every problem myself?

No. FixItYourself focuses on simple repairs. Serious or dangerous problems should be handled by a qualified technician.

Do I need an account?

You can explore the available information. Account requirements will depend on the features you want to use.

Safety First!

Always follow safety instructions before attempting a repair. If a problem is beyond your ability, seek help from a qualified technician.
